About

From financial statements to business decisions

Pratibha is currently pursuing a Post-Graduation Diploma in Quantitative Finance at Narayana Business School, following an Honours bachelor's degree at Shri Vaishnav Vidyapeeth Vishwavidhyalaya. Her academic and internship work sits at the intersection of financial analysis, corporate finance, and financial modelling.

As a Financial Analyst Intern at Coromandel International, she built a ten-year financial model, studied the effect of USD/INR volatility on margins and returns, and worked hands-on inside SAP FI/MM on vendor management, invoice verification, MIS reporting, and budgeting — direct exposure to how a corporate finance function actually runs.

Independently, she has completed a series of applied projects covering ratio analysis, DuPont and Altman Z-Score analysis, WACC and working capital assessment, and DCF valuation — consistently pairing Excel-based rigor with a clear business narrative.
